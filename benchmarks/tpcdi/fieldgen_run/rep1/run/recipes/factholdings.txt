Recipe for factholdings
Grain: One row per HoldingHistory record (HH_T_ID), i.e., one holding snapshot after each trade.
Combine: none
Branch b1:
  FROM HoldingHistory hh LEFT JOIN Trade t ON hh.HH_T_ID = t.T_ID
Decisions: joins: ['LEFT JOIN Trade t ON hh.HH_T_ID = t.T_ID']; stacked: False; deduplicated: False; aggregated: False
Assumption: HH_T_ID is the current trade identifier and HH_H_T_ID is the original trade identifier.
Assumption: HH_AFTER_QTY is the quantity held after the current trade (currentholding).
Assumption: Trade.T_TRADE_PRICE is the unit price for the current trade (currentprice).
Assumption: Trade.T_DTS provides the date and time for sk_dateid and sk_timeid.
Assumption: Trade.T_CA_ID is the natural key for the account dimension; Trade.T_S_SYMB is the natural key for the security dimension.
Assumption: Surrogate keys for customer, account, security, company, date, and time are obtained by joining to dimension tables in a later step; those dimension tables are not listed in the input and are not part of this recipe.
Assumption: BatchID for the historical load is constant 1.
Assumption: No de-duplication is needed because HH_T_ID is unique per row.
